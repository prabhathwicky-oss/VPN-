[Company name] ("we") operates the YPN VPN app. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## What we do not collect
We do not log or store the websites or apps you use, your DNS queries, the content of your traffic,
or your browsing history. We do not sell personal data.

## What we collect
- **Account ID** — an anonymous identifier created when you first open the app; your email only if you choose to save your account.
- **Device record** — app version, Android version and a random install ID, used to enforce device limits.
- **Connection metadata** — which of our servers you are assigned to, the VPN key/identifier for that session,
  the time of your last handshake, and total bytes transferred per day. Used to apply free-plan limits, balance
  server load and prevent abuse. Daily usage totals are deleted after 90 days; session records 7 days after they end.
- **Purchases** — Google Play subscription status and order ID, to activate Pro.
- **Advertising** — free users see ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers as described in Google's policy.

## How the VPN works
The app uses Android's VpnService to route your device traffic through an encrypted tunnel (WireGuard or
VLESS/REALITY) to our servers, which forward it to the internet.

## Your choices
You can delete your account and data by emailing [support email]. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play.
